Controls

PC: WASD move, 1–5 weapon slots (5 = rocket launcher, only 10% of players get one each life), R reload, Space jump, Ctrl (or C) crouch, hold right mouse button to aim/scope, hold Tab for the leaderboard, mouse look (click the game to lock the mouse), left button fire, arrows/Q/E turn without a mouse.
Phone: left thumb moves, right thumb looks, 🔥 fires (you can drag while holding it), ⬆ jumps.

Full screen

The game runs inside the Claude window, so the browser may refuse full screen (iPhone Safari does not allow it at all). Open the game in its own tab. On iPhone, "aA" → "Hide Toolbar" also helps.
